# ERFAN OXIGEN — Login & Customer UI Rework

- Customer login now validates the phone against the admin-registered customer list before generating an OTP.
- Customer session remains active until explicit logout. After logout, login is required again.
- Verified customer status is only used as account verification; it no longer bypasses the login step after logout.
- Customer login received a premium glass/hero visual treatment using the ERFAN OXIGEN logo and login hero image.
- Customer home received a premium customer dashboard with birthday gift, personal space cards, offers, notifications and account actions.
- Birthday gift remains available only on the customer's birthday and can be claimed once per year.
- XP/Level system remains removed from this version.
